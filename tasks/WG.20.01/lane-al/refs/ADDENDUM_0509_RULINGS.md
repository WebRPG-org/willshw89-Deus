## Ruling 1 - PixelLab prompts are short plain-language templates (supersedes "maximum pixel detail")
- Every PixelLab prompt is a **short, consistent, templated plain-language description**: who the character is (race, sex, look), what they wear and hold, the pose or motion, the view, and the style. Same wording pattern for every character; only the slot values change.
- **Prompts carry NO pixel coordinates, NO hex colour lists and NO hyperspecific pixel-level detail** (no row/column numbers, no `(dx,dy)` anchors, no head/torso/leg px counts, no "N px long/wide" object sizes, no "at most N colours", no per-frame "moves 1 px" instructions).
- **Size and palette are enforced by the tool settings plus post-processing**, not by the prompt: PixelLab canvas size, outline and detail settings; then the local post-process (crop/anchor to the 48 cell, height/width check against the chart, palette snap, per-frame weapon-length check, outline and grayscale checks). The chart numbers, ramps and weapon lengths stay in the standard as **post-process / QA data**, not prompt text.
- This **supersedes** the Owner 16:16 CT "maximum pixel-level control in every prompt" rule (standard R10) and every template built on it.
- Example of the intended pattern (PM illustration, Owner may edit): `human man, plain linen work clothes and leather boots, empty hands, standing relaxed, high top-down view, readable high-contrast fantasy pixel art` - attack clips add the held weapon and a short motion phrase, e.g. `swings a longsword in a rising diagonal slash`.

## Ruling 2 - Shields are not animated; weapon groups revised
- **Shields are not animated.** No shield appears in any character base, armor State or animation. Remove every "1H+shield" / "one-hand + shield" group and the `ATK_1H_SHIELD` clip. (PM reading: shields stay SRD items with their icon / world sprite / portrait entries; they just never show on the map sprite.)
- **Weapon groups (8):** unarmed, dagger, **one-hand sword (no shield)**, two-hand, spear/polearm, staff, bow, crossbow.
- **Animations per armor state: 19, all in 8 directions** = 8 weapon-group attacks + cast + 10 everyday (idle, walk, work, sleep, sit, eat/drink, carry, hurt, knocked down, dead). **Idle and walk 4 frames; all others 6.**
- Slot-ID ANIM code: `ATK_1H` (PM code) replaces `ATK_1H_SHIELD`; `ATK_1H_SHIELD` is **retired and reserved** (item 14: slot IDs are never reused). The one-hand group keeps the SRD one-hand weapons listed for it in standard 5.2 and draws a one-hand sword (longsword) with no shield.
