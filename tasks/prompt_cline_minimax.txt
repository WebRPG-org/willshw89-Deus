## 3. Background & Mathematical Calibration (DEC-038 Ratified)
1. **Stratum-Level Authority (2-ft vertical strata)**:
   - Groundwater operates at `(x, y, z, stratum)` for stratum $s \in [0..4]$ within each 10-ft Z cell.
   - Strata volume: $5\text{ ft} \times 5\text{ ft} \times 2\text{ ft} = 50\text{ cu ft}$ per stratum cell.
   - Depth bands: Deep Earth $[-16 \dots -11]$, Caverns $[-10 \dots -5]$, Lowlands $[-4 \dots +1]$, Uplands $[+2 \dots +6]$, Highlands $[+7 \dots +11]$, Sky $[+12 \dots +15]$.
   - Rock/material varies per stratum (sandstone, shale, limestone, granite, gravel).
   - Each stratum cell tracks:
     - Porosity $\phi$: integer basis points ($0 \dots 10000$, where $10000 = 100.00\%$).
     - Saturation $S$: integer basis points ($0 \dots 10000$).
     - Hydraulic conductivity $K$: integer scale $0 \dots 1,000,000$ ($0$ = impermeable granite/shale, $100,000$ = sandstone, $500,000$ = gravel, $1,000,000$ = max).
     - Hydraulic head $h$: integer millistrata ($1000\text{ units} = 1\text{ stratum} = 2\text{ ft}$).
2. **Global Elevation Datum & Hydraulic Head**:
   - Total hydraulic head $h = \text{elevationHead} + \text{pressureHead}$ relative to global bedrock ($Z=-16, s=0$ is datum 0):
     $$\text{elevationHead}(z, s) = ((z - (-16)) \times 5 + s) \times 1000\text{ millistrata}$$
3. **Interface Conductivity & Neighbor Darcy Stencil**:
   - Lateral groundwater transfer across adjacent cells uses the **harmonic mean** of permeabilities:
     $$K_{\text{interface}} = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } K_A = 0 \text{ or } K_B = 0 \\ \left\lfloor \frac{2 \cdot K_A \cdot K_B}{K_A + K_B} \right\rfloor & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
   - Flow follows hydraulic gradient: $Q \propto K_{\text{interface}} \cdot (h_A - h_B) / \text{distance}$.
4. **Canonical Undirected Edge & Signed Residual Tracking**:
   - Residual transfer flow MUST be tracked on canonical undirected interface edges (`signedResidual[edge(A,B)]` where `edge(A,B) = min(A,B) + "<->" + max(A,B)`):
   $$\text{signedGrossFlow} = \text{calculatedSignedFlow}_{A \to B} + \text{signedResidual}[\text{edge}(A,B)]$$
   $$\text{integerFlow} = \text{Math.trunc}(\text{signedGrossFlow})$$
   $$\text{signedResidual}[\text{edge}(A,B)] = \text{signedGrossFlow} - \text{integerFlow}$$
   - $\text{integerFlow} > 0 \implies \text{transfer from } A \to B$
   - $\text{integerFlow} < 0 \implies \text{transfer from } B \to A \text{ with magnitude } |\text{integerFlow}|$
   - Eliminates neighbor processing order bias and ensures flow reversal naturally cancels fractional remainder rather than preserving stale directional debt.
5. **Double-Sided Clamping Invariant on Every Transfer**:
   - Every discrete transfer must strictly clamp against both sides:
     $$\text{transfer} \le \text{donor.availableWater}$$
     $$\text{transfer} \le \text{receiver.availableCapacity}$$
   - Where $\text{availableCapacity}$ represents pore capacity for porous strata cells, or open fluid capacity for exposed cavern/void cells.
   - Strictly eliminates negative water, oversaturation past 10,000 basis points, and phantom mass.
6. **Event-Driven & Dirty-Region Cadence**:
   - Zero global per-tick full-world loops.
   - Resting subterranean aquifers in undisturbed rock are quiescent (static).
   - Breaches (excavations, collapse voids) mark adjacent aquifer cells as dirty.
   - Only dirty regions update during hydraulic equilibration cycles until steady state is reached.
7. **Mass Balance & Conservation**:
   - Water mass seeping into an open void decrements rock saturation and credits fluid volume in `game/js/sim/ledger.js`.
   - Authoritative unit: **integer centipounds** ($1\text{ unit} = 0.01\text{ lb}$). Net mass must be 100% conserved.
