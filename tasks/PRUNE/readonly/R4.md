## 3. In-Depth Architectural & Technical Analysis

### 3.1 Live Authorities (The 4 KEEP Files)
The four designated files describe core, tested, and actively executed systems:
1. **`DEUS_ZRange.md` (DEC-030 Authority)**:
   - Authored under WG.00.17 to implement the Owner's ratified 32-level vertical world ($-16..+15$, 320 ft elevation range, 2-ft strata resolution).
   - Authoritative consumer contract for `DEUS_World.js` (`UF.World.zRange()`), `DEUS_Levels.js` (sparse chunk storage, z-bound clamping), and `DEUS_Fluid.js` (vertical fluid propagation).
   - Verified by automated gate test `tools/test_zrange.js`. **Must remain in `docs/systems/`**.
2. **`DEUS_WaterDynamics.md` (Subterranean & Atmospheric Hydro Authority)**:
   - Describes the mass-conserved hydro physics required directly by `DEUS_Fluid.js:984` (`require("../sim/hydro/index.js")`).
   - Defines integer depth units (du), seepage rates based on material porosity (classes 0..6), column aquifers, and the atmospheric precipitation ledger.
   - Verified by `tools/sim/test_water_dynamics.js`. **Must remain in `docs/systems/`**.
3. **`DEUS_OcclusionCulling.md` (Presentation & Viewport Culling Authority)**:
   - Documents the WG.00.21 line-of-sight downward culling algorithm and bounded exposed-area rendering.
   - Prevents drawing occluded geometry and entities on lower Z levels when solid tiles block line of sight from the camera viewport.
   - Bound to `DEUS_Depth.js` and `DEUS_Culling.js`. **Must remain in `docs/systems/`**.
4. **`DEUS_Rules.md` (SRD 5.1 Combat & Mechanics Authority)**:
   - Documents the host-agnostic, deterministic D&D 5e / SRD 5.1 rules engine in `game/js/sim/rules/`.
   - Backed by `species_map.js`, deterministic d20 rolls, proficiency math, and bounded AC calculations. Loaded into live runtime by `DEUS_Combat.js`.
   - Verified by `tools/test_srd_rules_proof.js` and `tools/test_srd_combat_proof.js`. **Must remain in `docs/systems/`**.

### 3.2 Obsolete / Contradictory Architecture Specs
1. **`DEUS_PHYSICAL_WORLD_SIMULATION_SPEC.md`**:
   - **Contradiction**: Section 1 (line 17) explicitly specifies: *"digging, mining, structural support, building, roofs, fluids, fire, weather, and AI pathfinding operate on a single shared physical model across five persistent vertical Z-levels ($Z \in \{-2, -1, 0, 1, 2\}$)."*
   - Section 1.2 (line 21) dictates performance constraints around $256 \times 256 \times 5$ grids.
   - **Conflict**: Owner Decision **DEC-030** expanded the physical world from 5 levels to 32 levels ($-16..+15$). This document represents an earlier, un-migrated physical specification that conflicts with both `DEUS_ZRange.md` and the actual engine strata memory model in `DEUS_Levels.js`.
   - **Verdict**: **ARCHIVE**.
2. **`DEUS_VerticalBiomes.md`**:
   - **Contradiction**: Section 0 (line 5) asserts: *"The rock below Z-2 stays solid. Coupling only names the substrate there. Caves, pockets and fluids stay on the core levels -2..+2."*
   - Section 1 (line 9) explicitly states: *"DEC-013 item 7, the two underground bands. This is not DEC-030's later edge table (Deep Earth -16..-11, Caverns -10..-5, Lowlands -4..+1)."*
   - **Conflict**: The document directly admits that it rejects DEC-030 in favor of an obsolete pre-strata two-band model.
   - **Verdict**: **ARCHIVE**.

### 3.4 Stale UF_ Forwarder Docs
