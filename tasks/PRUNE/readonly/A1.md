## 1. Executive Summary & Critical Architectural Finding

1. **Origin of Parked Autotiles:**
   The autotiles inducted on `task/art-temperate-induction` (`90c82ac5`) were **not** inducted from the 55 PixelLab 16-variation transition tile sets (`tiles_pro_list.json`). Instead, they were constructed via algorithmic 96x144 autotile block synthesis (`buildA2Block` in `tools/art/process_meadow_variants.js`) using single 48x48 master PNGs from `art/masters/`.
   - **Meadow:** Sourced from the Owner's `art/masters/owner/2026-09-29_biome/` Set 0 (variant 0), verified against `process_meadow_variants.js`.
   - **Other 16 Terrains:** Sourced from individual 48x48 flash generations in `art/tilesets/individual_48/` dispatched via PixelLab MCP `create_image_pro_flash` (recorded in `art/tilesets/individual_48/tile_jobs.json`). Note that `generate_biome_tiles.js` and `create_image_pro_flash` were subsequently retired and banned per PM Directive 0175-U & DEC-007.

2. **PixelLab Tile-Set QA Coverage (MSG-PRUNE-PM-010):**
   The QA verdicts reported in MSG-PRUNE-PM-010 were an **approximate keyword-match heuristic** against PixelLab's 55 two-terrain transition tile-sets:
   - 7 terrains had keyword-matched proxy verdicts (`dry-grass`, `sand`, `swamp-mud`, `peak-rock`, `needle-floor`, `rock`, `forest-floor`).
   - 10 terrains (`meadow`, `dirt`, `mud`, `scree`, `shrub-soil`, `stony`, `road`, `cave-floor`, `dug-earth`, `mined-stone`) had **no QA data** in the 55 PixelLab transition tile sets.

3. **Status per MSG-PRUNE-PM-013:**
   As confirmed by PM (Claude) in MSG-PRUNE-PM-013:
   > *"Your autotile sources are art/masters files (`SURFACE_SHARED_TILE_*-{BASE,DAMP,DRY}`), not PixelLab tile sets, so the PixelLab tile verdicts do not cover them, and the repo group art_masters_loose was only judged by sample (MIXED). Claude is putting these 17 terrains through braintrust QA now. The parked branch stays parked until that verdict and the Owner's YEA."*
