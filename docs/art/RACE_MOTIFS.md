# Race motifs — the artistic identity of each people

Owner, 2026-09-29: "Each race needs a 'motif,' like an artistic motif, there should be some written down somewhere." This is the one place. It consolidates what already existed (banner devices from the 2026-09-29 racial banners, faction style profiles in `docs/art/DEUS_FACTION_ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD.md` §5, the peoples in `docs/design/PEOPLES.md`) under the world feel of VISION V152: Ultima VII, EverQuest, and English folklore (Owner, 2026-09-29: "Not english countryside, english folklore flavor to the game."). Every prompt for a people, its buildings, props and banner carries its motif line; the PM's board reviews accept or reject against it.

Each entry: **Motif** (one line that everything else serves) · **Root** (the folklore or history it draws on) · **Silhouette** · **Palette** (heraldic tinctures for the banner, everyday colours for clothes and homes) · **Device** (the heraldic charge on the war banner) · **Materials and buildings** · **Small details** · **Never** (what would break the motif).

Banner colours and devices follow the Owner's worldbuilding thread, as carried into lore v1 (Owner, 2026-09-29: "use your judgement"; PM decision to take the thread's set whole). The PM's earlier proposed swaps (white hart, acorn, boar's head) are withdrawn. The banners generated before the art restart are archived and get redrawn to this set. Everyday cloth colours are separate (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3).

## Human
- **Motif:** the English village and its lord: field, hearth, hedge and hall; patched, repaired, lived-in.
- **Root:** medieval English commoners, yeomen and manor lords; the frontier settlements of Ultima VII.
- **Silhouette:** upright, plain, hooded or coifed; tools not weapons in the hand.
- **Palette:** undyed wool browns and greys, madder red, woad blue, forest green; banner **crimson, a crowned lion rampant gold** (Owner's worldbuilding thread (lore v1), adopted 2026-09-29; matches the generated banner).
- **Scheme (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3):** oatmeal wool `#BAB095`, grey hose `#6E7A85`; identity woad blue `#356782`; value accent linen white `#EDE9DE`; bright steel, brown leather.
- **Device:** the crowned, rearing gold lion.
- **Materials and buildings:** oak and pine frame, fieldstone footings, daub and wattle, thatch or cedar shingle; steep gables, small shuttered windows, horseshoe over the door (§5.1).
- **Small details:** drying herbs under rafters, iron-banded barrels, the well with its winch, the central hearth with a pot crane.
- **Never:** plate armour as daily wear, saturated primaries, anything that reads as a fantasy uniform.

## Elf
- **Motif:** the fair folk of the greenwood: old, pale, quiet, and not quite safe.
- **Root:** the elves and sídhe of British and Irish lore, the white hart of the hunt, the hollow hill; not the sleek high elf of modern fantasy.
- **Silhouette:** tall, slender, long straight hair, cloaks that hang still; pointed ears; a stillness in the pose.
- **Palette:** grey-green, moss, silver-birch white, dusk lavender; banner **forest green, a crescent moon and a leaf silver** (Owner's worldbuilding thread (lore v1), adopted 2026-09-29).
- **Scheme (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3):** grey-green `#5D7C68` (dominant and identity), moss `#435A4B`; value accent birch white `#E2EFF8`; blackened iron, dark patinated bronze; dusk lavender only as a hood lining.
- **Device:** the crescent moon and leaf.
- **Materials and buildings:** living heartwood, shaped roots, willow wattle, bark shingle, polished riverstone; curved sweeps, tree platforms, root walkways, hangings instead of doors (§5.3). Under V152 the bioluminescent lanterns become rushlights and pale fungus, and the silver filigree becomes braided rush and horn.
- **Small details:** a circlet of braided rush, deerskin, a standing stone at the glade's edge, moss on everything old.
- **Never:** glowing runes, crystal architecture, leaf-shaped everything.

## Half-Elf
- **Motif:** the border people: of the village and of the wood, at home in neither, quick and useful in both.
- **Root:** the marches between settled land and the old forest; changeling stories.
- **Silhouette:** slight, sun-browned, faintly pointed ears, hood up, a pouch at the belt.
- **Palette:** russet, blue-grey, the human browns with one elven grey-green; banner **per pale teal and crimson, two hands clasped beneath a star** (Owner's worldbuilding thread (lore v1), adopted 2026-09-29; replaces the generated leaf-and-spearhead).
- **Scheme (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3):** russet `#834A34`, blue-grey `#597C93`; identity and value accent the pale blue-grey hood `#91B3CD`; iron, brown leather.
- **Device:** clasped hands beneath a star.
- **Materials and buildings:** no separate style: the human frame with an elven curve in the roofline; a tower house at a ford or a wood's edge; timber above, stone below.
- **Small details:** the pouch, the walking staff, a mended cloak, a fiddle or pipe.
- **Never:** a third invented culture; they borrow from the two.

## Dwarf
- **Motif:** the smiths of the northern isles: stone, iron, weight and permanence.
- **Root:** Norse-settled Orkney and the northern hills; broch and dry-stone; the folklore of the smith under the hill.
- **Silhouette:** short and very broad, heavy beard or heavy braids, apron, hobnailed boots; nothing tapers.
- **Palette:** granite grey, blue-grey wool, iron black, bronze (dark red and dark green dropped by the PM's colour-scheme proposal of 2026-09-29, `docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3, pending the Owner: four peoples already lean on red); banner **cobalt, a hammer and a pick above a mountain, bronze** (Owner's worldbuilding thread (lore v1), adopted 2026-09-29; the generated cobalt-and-bronze cloth matches).
- **Scheme (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3):** blue-grey wool `#3C474F`, iron black `#27272B`; identity bronze `#A2713F`; value accent polished bronze `#D0995C`; iron with bronze edging.
- **Device:** the hammer and pick above a mountain.
- **Materials and buildings:** dressed granite, basalt slab, dry-stone joints, iron bands, slate; low heavy roofs, cliff overhangs, trapezoid lintels, arrow-slits, runes on lintels (§5.2).
- **Small details:** iron rings in the beard, the anvil on a basalt block, anthracite braziers, furs on stone benches.
- **Never:** gold-and-gem opulence, Scottish-tartan cliché, a dwarf that is only a short human.

## Gnome
- **Motif:** the hedgerow folk: small, sharp-eyed, thrifty, red-capped; the bank under the hawthorn is home.
- **Root:** English garden and hedgerow spirits, hobs and brownies; not the tinker-gnome of D&D.
- **Silhouette:** very small and wiry, large nose, red felt cap, patched clothes, small boots.
- **Palette:** red cap, earth browns, leather, acorn and moss; banner **turquoise, a cogwheel with a central gem, brass** (Owner's worldbuilding thread (lore v1), adopted 2026-09-29; matches the generated brass gears).
- **Scheme (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3):** earth brown `#6B5A3E`, moss `#5B7353`; identity the red cap `#B5280D`; value accent the linen shirt `#CAC0AF`; dull iron.
- **Device:** the cogwheel with a central gem.
- **Materials and buildings:** burrows in banks and under roots, turf and wattle, a round door of driftwood planks, tiny brass fittings (a kettle, a lantern), never machinery.
- **Small details:** a wren, a snail shell, a thimble-sized pot, a hedgehog kept as a dog.
- **Never:** gears, goggles, clockwork, garden-gnome kitsch.

## Halfling
- **Motif:** the village at harvest: warm, round, well-fed, tidy; the good life defended.
- **Root:** the English farming village, the harvest home, the ale bench.
- **Silhouette:** short and round-faced, curly hair, bare feet, waistcoat or apron, a cap or shawl.
- **Palette:** honey wood, wheat gold, apple green, whitewash, red brick; banner **sunflower yellow and green, a hill with a round door and a wheat sheaf** (Owner's worldbuilding thread (lore v1), adopted 2026-09-29; the generated sheaf, honey pole and barrel base stay).
- **Scheme (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3):** apple green `#6C935D`, linen `#CAC0AF`; identity and value accent wheat gold `#F7C03D`; bright steel, honey leather.
- **Device:** the hill, the round door and the wheat sheaf (the sheaf alone where there is little room).
- **Materials and buildings:** whitewashed plaster over wattle, cob, red brick chimneys, turf and rounded thatch, round doors and porthole windows, picket fences (§5.4).
- **Small details:** preserve jars, copper kettles, patchwork, a pipe, a laden table.
- **Never:** grim colours, tall thin forms, martial props on the house.

## Half-Orc
- **Motif:** the marcher folk: the border hill-fort, the boar, the scar; strength kept for the wall.
- **Root:** the Welsh marches and the northern border reivers; the boar of the British bestiary.
- **Silhouette:** tall and heavily built, small lower tusks, close-cropped hair or a tight braid, sleeveless jerkin, scarred forearms.
- **Palette:** charcoal, blood red, grey-green skin, raw leather, bone; banner **blood-red, charcoal and bone-white, a clenched fist between tusks** (Owner's worldbuilding thread (lore v1), adopted 2026-09-29; the generated red-and-charcoal cloth and tusk finial stay).
- **Scheme (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3):** charcoal `#27272B`, raw leather `#734F2D`; identity and value accent bone `#C8C3B7`; blackened iron.
- **Device:** the clenched fist between tusks.
- **Materials and buildings:** earth bank and ditch, palisade of split oak, a turf-roofed longhouse, a watch-post on the ridge; rough but square, not the goblin's scrap.
- **Small details:** a boar-tusk lashing, a hide shield on the wall, smoke from the longhouse roof, a whetstone at the belt.
- **Never:** green-skinned savage cliché, skull piles, scrap-metal chaos (that is the goblin motif, §5.6).

## Dragonborn
- **Motif:** the heraldic wyrm made flesh: long, scaled, proud, ceremonial; the red dragon of the hill-fort.
- **Root:** the red dragon of Wales, the Lambton and Sockburn worms, dragons on church fonts and shields; the citadel as a hall of pride.
- **Silhouette:** tall and long-limbed, a long serpentine neck, a narrow wedge head with back-swept horns, a long thick tail; scales that catch the light.
- **Palette:** deep red or deep green scale, bronze, black basalt, terracotta, gold; banner **bronze-red, a coiled winged dragon gold** (Owner's worldbuilding thread (lore v1), adopted 2026-09-29; the generated bronze-red scale cloth and gold finial stay).
- **Scheme (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3):** the individual's scale (`#6D1109` red or `#26421C` green), terracotta cloth `#A16147`; identity gold `#BE891B`; value accent gold highlight `#FFB833`; black iron with bronze edging.
- **Device:** the coiled, winged gold dragon.
- **Materials and buildings:** dressed black basalt, red sandstone, bronze banding, terracotta crests; flared hipped roofs, wide tall doors for the snout and horns, podium terraces, dragon-head gargoyles, bronze braziers (§5.5).
- **Small details:** a scale-patterned cloth, a bronze tripod, claw-foot bases, fire kept burning.
- **Never:** the broad-chested Western fantasy dragon-man, feathers, neon.

## Tiefling
- **Motif:** the devil on the church wall: horns, ember and soot; a people carrying the medieval demon in their blood, not a costume.
- **Root:** the devils of English church carving, misericords, bestiaries and morality plays; the horned, goat- and bat-featured fiend of the doom painting.
- **Silhouette:** sharp features, two curved goat horns, small bat ears, a thin tail; black and red cloth, a deep hood.
- **Palette:** soot black, ember red and orange, ash grey, dull gold; banner **deep violet, a flame within a ring of horns, gold, trimmed in black iron** (Owner's worldbuilding thread (lore v1), adopted 2026-09-29; the generated pole, horn finial and ember base stay).
- **Scheme (`docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` §3):** soot `#212325`, ash grey `#59594F`; identity ember orange `#F26018`; value accent ember light `#DC906B`; blackened steel, dull gold.
- **Device:** a flame enclosed by a ring of horns.
- **Materials and buildings:** soot-dark brick and iron, chapel-like pointed arches, ember lanterns, a bell; well-built and old, never scrap.
- **Small details:** a bat, a goat, a bell, embers in a black iron dish, a doom-painting fragment.
- **Never:** purple-skinned modern tiefling, glamour, neon magic.

## The other peoples (VISION V87, `docs/design/PEOPLES.md`): proposals, not yet Owner-approved
- **Goblin:** the salvage camp (§5.6 stands): scrap, hide, bone, red handprints; root: the boggart and the robber band. Device: a red hand on sable.
- **Orc:** the raider host of the moor: hide, iron, black and rust; root: the giants and ogres of the northern tales. Device: a black wolf's head on gules.
- **Lizardfolk:** the fen-dwellers: reed, mud, rush-woven, the marsh nest; root: the knucker of the Sussex ponds, the fen lantern. Device: a green serpent nowed on argent.
- **Kobold:** the mine-vexers: small, doggish, under the hill with the dwarves' enemies; root: the knocker of the Cornish mines. Device: a pick and a candle on sable.
- **Undead:** the risen of barrow and gallows: bone, shroud, barrow-earth; root: the barrow-wight and the revenant of the churchyard. Device: none; they carry the banner of what they were, tattered.
- **Star-born:** pale polished stone with seams of light: the one people outside the British root by design; root: the "fortress of glass" of `docs/design/THEME.md` §A. Device: a mullet of eight points argent on azure.

## How this file is used
- Prompts: each base prompt for a people, its buildings and props ends with its motif line before the style tail (AS-LOOK-002): e.g. `… ; motif: the hedgerow folk, red cap, acorn, hawthorn bank`.
- Reviews: the PM's board review names the motif and rejects for feel when the asset serves another one (a tinker gnome, a fantasy-uniform human, a glowing elf).
- Outfits and colour schemes: `docs/art/RACE_OUTFITS.md` gives each people one drawing per outfit tier (basic clothes, robe, light, medium, heavy: the SRD armour categories), the colour scheme that fills `RAMP_RACE_<RACE>`, and the readability rules R1–R8 (Owner 2026-09-29: "Everything should be oriented towards readability").
- Banners: the device column is the acceptance standard for the nine banners in the all-assets pass; the gnome and elf devices are proposed changes for the Owner's word.
